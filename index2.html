About Our Business

Welcome to Star M Plaza Cafe and Restaurant

Situated in Belari, Star M Plaza is your premier destination for mouthwatering food, family gatherings, and special celebrations. Whether you crave rich Dum Biryani, spicy Chinese wok dishes, freshly baked Tandoori naans, or chilling mocktails, we serve quality with passion.

Beyond great dining, we proudly offer a fully equipped Marriage Hall (ম্যারেজ হল) for grand events, restful Guest House (গেস্ট হাউস) rooms, and an enjoyable Children's Park (চিলড্রেন পার্ক) so the whole family can relax together.
